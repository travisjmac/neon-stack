Neon Stack

A 10 by 20 well.Fill a row and it clears.

Controls

  • LeftRight Move

  • Space Hard drop
  • UpX Rotate clockwise
  • ZCtrl Counterclockwise
  • C Hold
  • P Pause
  • R Restart
  • M Mute
  • Esc Quit

On a narrow screen the pad under the well is move, soft drop, rotate counterclockwise, hard drop, and hold. A ghost shows the hard-drop landing. Hold once, then wait until that piece locks.

Those points are for level 1. Multiply by the level. Soft drop is 1 point a cell. Hard drop is 2, and it locks. Every 10 lines the fall and the music speed up.

A clear plays a whoosh, then a boom. Two booms when several lines clear.
